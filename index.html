How we work

Proof first. The big money later.

The first two stages are a matter of weeks. The pilot is four weeks to several months depending on how many systems and exception paths the process really has — and we would rather say that now than halfway through. Every stage has an end, an output and a decision, so at every one of them you can say stop.

1 Stage 1 Find where it actually hurts We map the volume of work, the exceptions, the data, and what today's way of doing it really costs you. 1–2 weeks
2 Stage 2 Work out whether it pays Baseline, expected saving, risks, and a clear condition for calling the pilot a success. 2–3 weeks
3 Stage 3 Build the pilot on real data We connect it to the real systems and measure the benefit on real work, not on a demo. 4 weeks to several months
4 Stage 4 Take over operations Monitoring, security, SLA and further development. It has a specific owner, and that owner has a name. ongoing, for years
